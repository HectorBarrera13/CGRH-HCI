# Project Overview

## UADY.10.25 – UADY Benefits System  
**General Coordination of Human Resources (CGRH) – Universidad Autónoma de Yucatán**

---

## Context

The UADY Benefits System is a web-based self-service platform developed for the General Coordination of Human Resources (CGRH) of the UADY. Its purpose is to provide university staff with autonomous and centralized access to their payroll documents and savings funds, eliminating reliance on in-person or intermediated procedures.

The system is designed for the entire active and retired university workforce, and covers the following functional modules: registration and login, consultation of Digital Tax Receipts (CFDI), consultation of the Retirement Savings Fund (FAR), and consultation of the Savings Fund.

## HCI Project Focus

For the Human-Computer Interaction course, the analysis and design is scoped to the segment of **retired employees over 60 years of age**, considered the user profile with the highest density of technological access barriers within the system's target population. This delimitation is methodologically advantageous: design criteria derived from the most demanding profile benefit the system as a whole.

The functional requirements selected for the HCI analysis are:

| ID | Name |
|---|---|
| RF01 | Employee Registration |
| RF02 | Password Recovery |
| RF03 | Login |
| RF04 | Logout |
| RF05 | Main Menu |
| RF10 | CFDI Consultation |
| RF20 | Retirement Savings Fund Consultation |
| RF30 | Savings Fund Consultation |

---

## General Objective

To analyze and propose usability and accessibility improvements for the UADY Benefits System, applying Human-Computer Interaction principles and methodologies, with a focus on retired employees over 60 years of age, in order to ensure the system is accessible, understandable, and autonomously usable by this user profile.

---

## Specific Objectives

1. **Research the target user** through qualitative and quantitative research techniques (interviews, surveys, and contextual inquiry) to characterize the technology usage patterns, needs, and digital access barriers of UADY's retired employees.

2. **Build user models** (profiles, personas, and scenarios) grounded in collected data, representing the retiree segment as the primary HCI user and guiding design decisions throughout the process.

3. **Evaluate the interaction flows** of the selected functional requirements (RF01–RF05, RF10, RF20, RF30) against usability, cognitive accessibility, and interaction load criteria, identifying friction points and improvement opportunities.

4. **Document usability requirements** (non-functional) aligned to HCI attributes relevant to the retiree profile: navigational simplicity, system feedback, error handling, readability, and visual consistency.

5. **Propose and prototype design improvements** for the system's critical views, prioritizing the flows with the highest impact for the retired user: registration (RF01), password recovery (RF02), and login (RF03).

---

## HCI Analysis Scope

- **Platform:** Desktop web application
- **User analyzed:** UADY retired employees, over 60 years of age
- **Requirements covered:** RF01, RF02, RF03, RF04, RF05, RF10, RF20, RF30
- **Out of scope:** Active employees, beneficiaries, system administrators, and modules not listed (e.g., Housing Fund)
