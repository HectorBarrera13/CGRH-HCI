# AITools

I am really surprised about what we can do in a few seconds with the AI Tools, I just send a prompt to Deep Seek asking to create an WebApp named MyArt, I wrote to Deep Seek that just do the UI, it wasn't need to be functional, and I explained to him that the App basically was a Marketplace where you can sell your art and the main public was the small artist.

It take him just one or two minutes to generate an file in HTML(including CSS and JS); when I run it, I was shocked about the results, the page looks really good, maybe it could be a little generical, but the parts of the page are where they must be, there is the main bar on the top, the name of the page on the top-left, and in the top-right are the icons of shopping, represented by a shopping cart logo; then favorites, represented by a heart; and the profile photo, that we assume, leads to profile configurations.

As I said, in general all the elements are in the right place, even Deep Seek generated a complete footer that perfectly could be the footer of any serious page, with a short description of the page objectives, quick links, information for the people how wants to sell their art in the page, and contact data. It was really easy to do, and the prompt wasn't the most explained one, I just give the general context and the AI did the rest.

The usability of the page is pretty good, actually, even I'm a new user, I didn't know nothing about the page, and I didn't give any suggestion of how it should look, and I´m capable of recognize where are all the things that a Market place like this should have and I can find easily where there are; if I were an user who wants to sell his art, I would know what I need to do, at least the first steps that are yet represented in the interface generated; and if I were a client who wants to buy an piece of art, I would know how to complete the process to buy it, at least the first steps again.

It will definitely be changes in the UX role, maybe in the past there was a whole team that was responsible of do the interface, now one person with the help of the AI can do the whole interface, I just did it without having the best UX knowledge and the interface that the AI just generated to me was pretty good. So maybe that will be the new role of "UX Engineer", just fallow the Nielsen´s usability principles whit the help of AI tools, but guiding the process with him knowledges.
