# AI Tools

I have been using chat gpt and claude to generate the components and the css/tailwind of my code, and I think it is great. It generates a good quality product for a basic project and it saves me a lot of time because I use it as a base and with the knowledge I have I change and adjust it as I want.

In terms of quality and usability, these tools provide a very solid foundation. The UI produced is clean and modern, but I’ve noticed that it sometimes lacks deep accessibility features, like proper ARIA labels or specific focus states. While the AI is incredibly easy to use and handles the heavy lifting of CSS positioning, the 'usability attribute' still depends on my intervention to ensure the flow makes sense for a real human. Ultimately, I see the UX role shifting from manual building to high-level auditing, where our expertise is needed to refine what the AI generates.
