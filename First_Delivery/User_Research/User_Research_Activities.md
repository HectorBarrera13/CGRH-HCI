# User Research – Activities and Justification

## General Human Resources Coordination System (CGRH – UADY)

### Project Context

The CGRH system is designed to be used by all employees of the Universidad Autónoma de Yucatán (UADY). However, for the purposes of this academic project, the User Research process will focus exclusively on retired employees over 60 years of age, who present specific characteristics such as basic or low digital literacy, possible visual decline, and difficulty remembering passwords.

This focus will allow a deeper analysis of the needs of a vulnerable group and ensure that the system meets accessibility, simplicity, and ease-of-use criteria for core functions such as login and consultation of financial and administrative information (CFDI, Retirement Savings Fund, and Savings Fund).

---

## Selected User Research Activities

### 1. Interviews

**Justification:**  
Interviews will be used to understand the needs, limitations, and expectations of retired employees regarding the use of the CGRH system. This method will provide detailed qualitative information through a guided dialogue, using clear language adapted to the user profile.

**Expected outcomes:**

- Main difficulties when using digital systems
- Prior experience with institutional platforms
- Needs related to access and password recovery
- General expectations about the CGRH system

---

### 2. Contextual Inquiry

**Justification:**  
Contextual observation will be applied to analyze how users interact with the system in their real environment, whether from a personal or workplace computer. This method will identify problems that users might not mention directly during interviews.

**Expected outcomes:**

- Information about the real usage context
- Physical or cognitive barriers during interaction
- Difficulties related to visibility and navigation
- Dependence on external assistance to complete tasks

---

### 3. Surveys

**Justification:**  
Surveys will be used to collect general information from a broader group of retired employees, enabling identification of common usage patterns and recurring difficulties. Questions will be designed with simple language and a clear structure to facilitate comprehension.

**Expected outcomes:**

- Most commonly used devices among users
- Frequency of use of system functions
- Main reported problems
- General level of comfort with the system

---

### 4. Usability Testing

**Justification:**  
Usability tests will be conducted to evaluate how users perform key tasks in the CGRH system, such as registration, login, password recovery, and financial information consultation. This method will validate the functional requirements defined for the system.

**Expected outcomes:**

- Most frequent errors during critical tasks
- Approximate time to complete each task
- Comprehension problems with messages and buttons
- Evidence of accessibility and usability issues

---

## Expected Outcomes of the User Research Process

- Early identification of usability problems
- Clear understanding of the target user's needs
- Sufficient information for the creation of personas and scenarios
- Solid foundation for refining functional requirements
- Documented evidence to justify design decisions
