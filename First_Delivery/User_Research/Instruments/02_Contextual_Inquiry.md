# Contextual Inquiry Protocol
## Activity 2: Contextual Observation — CGRH UADY

**Prepared by:** Damián Villares
**Date:** February 27, 2026
**Target profile:** Retired UADY employees, over 60 years of age

---

## 1. Activity Description

Contextual Inquiry is a qualitative research technique that combines observation and interviewing in the user's real environment. The researcher observes the participant while they perform tasks with the system (or similar tasks on familiar platforms), asking questions in the moment when behaviors or difficulties of interest arise.

Since the CGRH system is still under development, the session will be structured in two phases:

- **Phase 1 (Free observation):** The participant interacts with a digital platform they already use (e.g., their bank's website, WhatsApp Web, or the SAT portal if applicable).
- **Phase 2 (Prototype or mockup):** If a low or medium fidelity prototype is available, the participant attempts to complete specific tasks within the CGRH system.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Estimated duration** | 20 – 30 minutes per session |
| **Modality** | In-person at the participant's home or at an agreed location |
| **Participant profile** | Retired UADY employee, over 60 years old, basic level of digital literacy |
| **Number of participants** | 3 – 5 participants |
| **Team roles** | 1 lead researcher (conducts the session) + 1 observer (takes notes) |
| **Required materials** | Desktop computer, audio/video recorder (with consent), observation log sheets |

---

## 2. Pre-Session Preparation

### 2.1 Logistics coordination

- Contact the participant at least 3 days in advance to confirm the date, time, and location.
- Verify that the session location has a desktop computer available and an internet connection.
- Prepare the informed consent form and bring it printed.
- Prepare the prototype or mockup if available; verify that it works correctly before the session.
- Bring a field notebook, pencil, printed observation sheets, and a recorder (if applicable).

### 2.2 Environment setup

- Ask the participant to use their usual computer exactly as they would normally find it.
- Do not organize or clean the participant's desktop: any clutter or icons present are relevant contextual information.
- Position yourself slightly behind and to the side of the participant to observe the screen without interfering with their movement.

---

## 3. Session Protocol

### Phase 0 – Opening and Consent (10 min)

**Suggested script:**

> "Thank you for having me. Today I would like to observe you while you use the computer to do a few everyday things. I am not evaluating whether you do things right or wrong; I am evaluating the system in order to improve its design. While you work, I will ask you to think out loud: to tell me what you are thinking or what you expect to happen when you do something. Does that sound okay?"

---

### Phase 1 – Observation on a Familiar Platform (20 min)

Ask the participant to perform a task they already do regularly on the computer. Examples:

- Open WhatsApp Web and send a message
- Check their bank account balance online
- Search for information on Google

During the observation, take note of the following dimensions:

| Observation dimension | Indicators to record |
|---|---|
| **Navigation** | How do they locate elements on screen? Do they use the scroll bar? Do they browse through menus or from memory? |
| **Reading** | Do they adjust the zoom? Do they read aloud? Do they ask for confirmation of what they read? |
| **Errors** | Do they click in the wrong places? Do they go back? Do they accidentally close windows? |
| **External support** | Do they call someone? Do they get up to look for something written down? Do they ask the researcher? |
| **Verbalizations** | What do they say out loud? Do they express confusion, frustration, or relief? |
| **Response time** | Do they pause before acting? Do they act quickly but incorrectly? |

---

### Phase 2 – Interaction with the CGRH System / Prototype (30 min)

If a prototype is available, ask the participant to complete the following tasks. Remember: **do not give instructions on how to do them**. Only state the objective.

| # | Requested task | Related RF |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create a new account in the system | RF01 – Registration |
| 2 | Log in to the system with their username and password | RF03 – Login |
| 3 | Recover their password if they forgot it | RF02 – Password recovery |
| 4 | Find and download their most recent payroll receipt | RF10 – CFDI consultation |
| 5 | Check the balance of their Retirement Savings Fund | RF20 – FAR consultation |
| 6 | Log out of the system | RF04 – Logout |

---

### Phase 3 – Debrief (10 min)

At the end of the session, ask the following closing questions:

1. How did you feel while using the system?
2. Was there anything you found confusing or difficult?
3. What did you find easiest?
4. Would you change anything about the way it works?
