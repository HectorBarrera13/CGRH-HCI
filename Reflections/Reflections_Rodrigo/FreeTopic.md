# Nielsen's Heuristics. Are They Still Relevant Today?

Last class we talked about the 10 usability heuristics by Jakob Nielsen, and then the professor asked us if that philosophy has changed nowadays.

I found Eleken, an agency that produces Software as a Service (SaaS) focused on UI and UX design, which proposed 9 heuristics last year. In short, they leverage explainable AI to make things easier for the user by explaining actual situations instead of relying only on a human employee. When clients reach out to them, AI is almost always part of the conversation, acting as a guide.

This is very interesting because, while reading their philosophy, I noticed they were using Nielsen's heuristics adapted to AI facilitating things like suggestions, feedback, and system status communication. At this point I understood that I wasn't trying to say that Jakob Nielsen's heuristics were outdated; they're just old.

Finally, I can say that this company uses heuristics very similar to Nielsen's, which confirms that they are still applicable in modern workspaces.
