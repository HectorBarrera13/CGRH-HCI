### AI Tools

An organization that recently uses AI for usability is Figma, I won't explain how it works because we are familiarized by this app. What I saw from this IA is that produces simply content like colors, letters and styles, but it is really generic, there is no innovation or creativity but it works, so I considered it a nice quality.

It was clearly easy to use AI in Figma, when I used it felts like I was having a technique conversation instead of clicking and dragging things, but just like other AI tools, the challenge is to make proper prompts to get better results and it was so quick that I could have many ideas or designs in a matter of minutes.

This AI tool is consistent, just like many AI tools that responds to your prompts, but it can make some errors. In few words, this AI acts like other AI Tools, It works quickly and does what it's supposed to, but it's prone to errors, so it needs human help, failing in Error Prevention and Accessibility.

It is known that the role of UX will change with AI, where this Tool will help us with the heavy artillery, provided we know how to use it, because it will be useless otherwise, where in Figma, the designer no longer draws, instead the designer directs the AI how things will need to be done.
