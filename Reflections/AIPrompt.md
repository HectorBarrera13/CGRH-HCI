## AI Prompt

The point of this reflection is that the AI as a tool can help us to have better
and more accurate results. For this activity, i decide to use ChatGPT as the AI
tool, I explored the usability attribute Intuitive Navigation. By creating a prompt
and analyzing the response, I was able to quickly obtain a structured set of metrics
that helped clarify what kind of data should be collected, such as user behavior,
efficiency, and perception. This shows that AI can be very effective in providing a
clear starting point and reducing ambiguity when defining evaluation criteria.

Honestly, even when working with my team, we rely on AI to guide our thinking and
generate initial ideas. This significantly reduces the time needed to define
usability testing tasks compared to doing everything manually. Without AI, the
process would require much more discussion, and iteration before reaching a solid
proposal. However, we also make sure to review and analyze the AI's suggestions
carefully, verifying that they actually make sense within the context of our system
and adjusting them when necessary.

At the same time, it is important to recognize that AI is only a tool and not a
replacement for critical thinking. While it can provide useful suggestions and help
us to have a faster process, it cannot fully understand the specific needs of a
project or make final decisions on its own. The responsibility still lies with us
to interpret, validate, and refine the information it generates. The best approach
is to use AI as support for analysis, combining its speed and structure with human
judgment to achieve more accurate and reliable usability testing results.

I used DeepSeek, and the promt I send was: I'm working on a Human-Computer
Interaction assignment. What steps do I need to take to collect the data required
for the "Intuitive Navigation" usability attribute?
