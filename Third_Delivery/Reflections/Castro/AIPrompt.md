# AI Prompt for Usability Testing Tasks

While we were designing the usability test for the platform, we wanted to ensure that the tasks we created would correctly measure the intuitive navigation and memorability of the system for our users. To achieve this, we used Claude by Anthropic to generate a set of concrete usability test tasks based on the description of the app and the attributes we defined last delivery.

Our goal with this was to get a draft of tasks that we could then refine and adapt to our specific context using our own knowledge of the users and the system. The AI-generated tasks provided a solid starting point, and we were able to modify them to better fit the unique characteristics of our user base and the platform's features.

This way of working allowed us to use the strengths of AI in generating structured and relevant content while still applying our human knowledge to ensure the tasks were realistic and meaningful for our specific usability testing needs. I think this process of combining AI-generated content with human refinement is a very effective approach and helps us produce quality content way faster than if we were to do it all from scratch using our Artisanal Intelligence. 

I think that this way of working is the future in a lot of fields as it doesn't replace the human element but helps it by creating a foundation we can then work upon. It doens't take away the creativity and critical thinking that humans have, but it does save us time and effort in generating initial ideas and content. This way, we dont become dependent on AI, but we can use it as a tool to enhance our own capabilities and productivity.

**AI Tool:** Claude Sonnet 4.5 (Anthropic)

**Prompt used:**

> I am designing a usability test for a web-based HR platform used by administrative and academic staff at a public university in Mexico. The system allows users to access and download their payroll receipts (CFDIs) and view their Fondo de Retiro, Caja de Ahorro and Fondo de Vivienda balances and statements in table views. The target users are non-technical employees, mostly between 35 and 60 years old, who access the platform infrequently — some check their payroll biweekly, but others only consult the savings fund section every 6 months. You should have memory of this app from our previous conversations. If you have any questions about the app or the users, please ask before generating the tasks.
>
> The usability attribute I am testing is **Intuitive Navigation**, with particular emphasis on **memorability**: the system must be easy to navigate even after a long period of inactivity, without requiring users to re-learn the interface from scratch.
>
> Please generate a set of 5 concrete usability test tasks that would allow me to collect data on how intuitively users can navigate the system after a period of absence. For each task, include: (1) a realistic scenario framing that puts the user in context, (2) the specific action the user must perform, and (3) the type of usability data or metric the task would help collect (for example: time on task, number of errors, number of clicks to complete the action, or verbal confusion indicators).
