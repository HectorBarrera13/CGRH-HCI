## Traceability Attributes

We picked Navigability as our main attribute at this moment, navigability was integrated into the prototype through structural decisions, information was organized around user goals, such as Mis Recibos and Mis beneficios, rather than system logic, persistent navigation paths and a consistent back function were included on every screen, and mobile navigation was reduced to a simple bottom tab bar to minimize disorientation.

To measure it, the team used metrics from the documented framework, task success rate and time on task benchmarked against an expert user captured effectiveness and efficiency, unnecessary clicks and navigation error count diagnosed where and how users lost their way. Qualitatively, perceived orientation added a Likert item on perceived orientation and error recovery observed error recovery behavior, all of this was directly related to the most likely failure modes for users unfamiliar with institutional portals.

Alternative data could measure the same attribute, such as first-click accuracy or disorientation score, but this data depends on human factors, that's why AI provided some initial ideas, as I mentioned in another reflection, but from there we began to add and modify especially the qualitative aspects.
