## Instructions for the Note-Taker

This sheet is completed during the session, one sheet per participant. It captures two metrics in parallel for every task:

**Unnecessary clicks (NAV-03):** Count every click the participant makes that is not part of the optimal path to complete the task. This includes clicking into the wrong section, clicking a button that does nothing relevant, clicking back to retrace steps, or clicking the same element more than once out of confusion. Do not count deliberate exploratory clicks on the first task — if the participant has never seen the system, one or two exploratory clicks at the start are expected. From Task 2 onward, count all deviations from the optimal path.

The optimal path (minimum number of clicks to complete each task) is defined in the **Benchmark** document. Keep that document open alongside this sheet during the session.

**Help requests (APR-03):** Count each time the participant explicitly stops attempting the task and asks the moderator a question, waits for direction, or verbalizes that they do not know how to continue and are expecting an answer. Do not count thinking aloud, expressions of frustration, or questions the participant answers themselves immediately after asking. Only count moments where the participant has stopped and is waiting for the moderator to respond.

Record each instance as it happens — do not estimate after the session.
