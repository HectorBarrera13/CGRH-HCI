## Purpose of This Document

This guide supports the note-taker in capturing qualitative observations during and after each usability testing session. It focuses on two metrics that cannot be reduced to a count or a timestamp:

**NAV-06 — Error Recovery:** When a participant makes an error, does the system give them enough information to recover on their own? This metric tracks not just whether an error occurred, but what happened next — did the participant understand what went wrong, did they find their way back, or did they stop entirely?

**APR-05 — Perceived Consistency:** Does the participant feel that the system behaves predictably from one section to the next? Does what they learn in one flow transfer naturally to another, or does each section feel like a new system? This metric is collected primarily through post-session conversation, not during tasks.

Both metrics require the note-taker to write descriptive observations in their own words — not just checkboxes. The value of this document is in the specificity of what is recorded. A note that says "user got confused" is not useful. A note that says "user clicked on 'Fondo de Retiro' instead of 'Fondo de Ahorro', read the page for 10 seconds, then said 'this doesn't look right' and used the back button without prompting" is.

---

## Instructions for the Note-Taker

**During tasks — NAV-06:**
Every time a participant makes a navigational or input error, open a new error event block in the corresponding task section. Record what happened, what the system showed in response, and how the participant reacted. Classify the recovery outcome using the three-level scale defined below. Do this in real time — do not wait until after the task ends.

**After all tasks — APR-05:**
This metric is collected during the post-task debrief conversation, not during tasks. The moderator will ask the participant two open questions about their experience with the system's consistency. The note-taker records the participant's response verbatim or as close to verbatim as possible. Do not summarize during the session — summarize only in the analysis column after the session ends.

**Recovery outcome scale (NAV-06):**

| Level | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| A | Independent recovery | Participant recognized the error and corrected course without any moderator input |
| B | Prompted recovery | Participant recovered after a neutral moderator prompt ("¿Qué estás pensando?") but without directional guidance |
| C | No recovery | Participant did not recover — either abandoned the task, repeated the error, or required the moderator to intervene beyond a neutral prompt |
