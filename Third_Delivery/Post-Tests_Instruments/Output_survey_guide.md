## Purpose of This Document

This guide structures the exit interview conducted at the end of each usability testing session, after the SUS questionnaire and the Orientation & Learning Likert Scale have been completed. It targets one metric that neither quantitative logs nor closed-scale instruments can capture directly:

**APR-06 — Short-term retention:** whether the participant can describe, in their own words and without prompting, how they would complete the system's core tasks again. If a participant can explain the steps clearly after a single session, the system's structure has been understood well enough to be retained. If they cannot, the interface requires more cognitive effort to process than users can realistically sustain across infrequent visits.

This metric is especially relevant for this system because the usage pattern identified in the research phase is inherently infrequent — payroll receipts are checked biweekly and savings fund balances every six months. A system that requires re-learning every time it is used will not be adopted.

---

## Instructions for the Moderator

Administer this interview after both post-test questionnaires have been collected. The tone should shift from structured to conversational — this is a dialogue, not a form.

Before starting, say:

> *"Ya terminamos con los cuestionarios. Ahora solo quiero platicar contigo unos minutos sobre tu experiencia. No hay respuestas correctas — me interesa lo que tú recuerdas y lo que sentiste."*

Read each question exactly as written. If the participant gives a very short answer, use the follow-up probe provided to invite elaboration. Do not suggest answers, correct the participant, or indicate whether their description of a task is accurate — the point is to observe what they retained, not to teach them.

The note-taker records responses verbatim or as close to verbatim as possible. Do not summarize during the session. Analysis is completed after.

Estimated duration: 10–15 minutes.

## Section 1 — Task Retention (APR-06)

These questions ask the participant to explain how they would complete each core task again, as if explaining it to a colleague who has never used the system. The moderator does not confirm or correct — only listens and prompts for elaboration when needed.

### Closing

Once all questions have been answered, the moderator closes the session with:

> *"Eso es todo, muchas gracias por tu tiempo. Lo que nos compartiste hoy es muy valioso para mejorar el sistema. Si tienes alguna pregunta sobre el proyecto puedes escribirnos al correo del equipo."*

Do not provide feedback on the participant's performance during the session. Do not reveal which tasks were completed successfully or which errors were recorded.
