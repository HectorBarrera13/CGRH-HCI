## Instructions for the Moderator

Administer this scale immediately after the SUS questionnaire, before the open debrief conversation. Hand the printed form to the participant and read the following instructions aloud:

> *"Este es un segundo cuestionario corto, también sobre tu experiencia con el sistema. Al igual que el anterior, no hay respuestas correctas o incorrectas. Para cada afirmación, elige un número del 1 al 5 según qué tan de acuerdo estás con lo que se describe. Basa tus respuestas en lo que sentiste durante las tareas que acabas de hacer."*

If the participant asks for clarification on a specific item, say: *"Marca lo que mejor describa tu experiencia durante la sesión."* Do not rephrase the item or give examples that could bias the response.

This scale targets two specific usability attributes that the SUS does not isolate with sufficient granularity for the purposes of this study:

- **NAV-05 — Perceived orientation:** whether the participant always knew where they were within the system and how to move forward or backward at any point.
- **APR-04 — Perceived ease of learning:** whether the participant felt the system was understandable from the first interaction, without prior training or explanation.

## Scoring (completed by the team after the session)

All items are positively worded — higher scores indicate better perceived orientation and learnability. No conversion is needed. Record the raw response for each item.

### NAV-05 — Perceived Orientation Score

| Item | Raw score |
|---|---|
| 1 — Supe dónde estaba | |
| 2 — Supe cómo regresar | |
| 3 — Nombres me ayudaron | |
| 4 — No me sentí perdido | |
| **Sum (max 20)** | |
| **Average (sum ÷ 4)** | |

> **Threshold: average ≥ 4.0 out of 5**
> An average below 4.0 indicates that participants consistently felt disoriented within the system — a direct signal that the navigation structure, labeling, or wayfinding cues need redesign.
