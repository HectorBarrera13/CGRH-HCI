## Instructions for the Moderator

Administer this questionnaire immediately after the participant completes all tasks, before the debrief conversation. Hand the printed form to the participant and read the following instructions aloud:

> *"Este es un cuestionario corto sobre tu experiencia usando el sistema. No hay respuestas correctas ni incorrectas — solo marca lo que sentiste mientras lo usabas. Para cada pregunta, elige un número del 1 al 5, donde 1 significa que estás completamente en desacuerdo y 5 significa que estás completamente de acuerdo. Por favor responde todas las preguntas."*

Do not explain or rephrase individual questions if the participant asks. If they express confusion, simply say: *"Marca lo que te parezca más cercano a tu experiencia."*

## Scoring (completed by the team after the session)

For even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): subtract the participant's response from 5.

## Score Interpretation

| Score | Grade | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 90 | A | Best imaginable |
| 80 – 89 | B | Excellent |
| 68 – 79 | C | Good — industry average |
| 51 – 67 | D | Poor |
| ≤ 50 | F | Unacceptable |

> **Minimum acceptable threshold for this system: 68**
> **Design target given user profile: ≥ 75**
>
> A score below 68 indicates the system does not meet basic usability standards and requires significant redesign before release. A score between 68 and 74 is acceptable but should be accompanied by targeted improvements in the flows that generated the most errors during the session.
