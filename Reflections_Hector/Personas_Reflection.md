# Internal University System Planning

In the planning phase of our internal university system—designed to help employees and retirees manage their CFDIs, personal files, and benefits—the Personas method is being applied as a strategic design tool that transcends traditional demographic segmentation by foregrounding motivations, anxieties, and behavioral patterns.

### Methodology: iXDF and Lene Nielsen
We are scoping our Persona construction around the "Needs" category, with deliberate attention to two critical toolkit items:

* **Support Requirements:** The type, frequency, and format of assistance users need to complete tasks independently.
* **Interface Trust Levels:** The degree to which a user believes the system is reliable, safe, and within their control.

These are particularly decisive variables when designing for older adults and retirees with varied digital literacy.

### Data Gathering and Synthesis
To ensure our Personas reflect real-world context, data will be gathered through:
1. **Contextual Inquiry:** Observing users within their actual administrative environments.
2. **Affinity Diagrams:** Structuring data to surface emergent themes and unmet needs.

### Impact on Design Decisions
The direct impact is concrete: interface elements like abstract iconography (cloud-upload or generic document symbols) will be replaced with large, plain-language action buttons like **"Download my Monthly Payment Receipt"**. 

This change is driven by persona findings regarding low interface trust and the cognitive load associated with technical visual metaphors. This positions the Persona as a living design argument that justifies every structural decision in the product.
