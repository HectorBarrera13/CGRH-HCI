# Using Personas

I want to argue that not all the functions of a system have the same importance to the end user. Last block we used proto-personas instead of real Personas based on user research, so we found many difficulties while we were applying surveys with real people in our project. We made Proto-personas focused on retired employees over 60 years old and, at the moment I was designing the survey, I found irregularities on what are we looking as an objective to this specific users that we were going to ask because the usability attributes changed depending on whether it was a retired or active employee.

Based on that, I can say that the knowledge applied of Personas to the UI/prototype last delivery was useless, Damian had to make another Personas based on our audience of interest because we were prioritizing functions of passwords and login instead of ease of use and navigation. When interviewing active users, they focused more on navigation rather than logging in, so we had to change the prioritization of system functions to that.
